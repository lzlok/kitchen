# Seafood stock
{: .no_toc }

Seafood stock can be a light broth made with fish bones and heads or a richer bisque made from the heads and shells of scampi, shrimp, or lobster.

## Light fish stock

### Ingredients

- Onion, carrot, and celery, roughly chopped
- Olive oil
- Fish bones and heads, chopped into manageable pieces
- 1 glass white wine
- Water
- Salt, about 1–2% by weight
- Whole peppercorns and bay leaves (optional)

### Instructions

1. Cook the onion, carrot, and celery in olive oil.
2. Add the fish bones and heads. Raise the heat to high and roast until they take on some color.
3. Pour in the white wine and allow the alcohol to evaporate for a couple of minutes.
4. Add enough water to cover the bones, season with salt, and add peppercorns or bay leaves if using.
5. Simmer for about 1 hour, then strain.

For a simple appetizer, serve the strained stock with freshly squeezed lemon juice.

## Crustacean bisque

### Ingredients

- Onion, carrot, and celery
- Olive oil
- Scampi, shrimp, or lobster heads and shells
- 1 tablespoon tomato concentrate (preferred), or a bunch of cherry tomatoes, halved
- 1 glass cognac or brandy; white wine also works
- Ice, or a mixture of ice and water

### Instructions

1. Begin with a soffritto of onion, carrot, and celery.
2. Add the crustacean heads and shells with the tomato concentrate or cherry tomatoes. Raise the heat to high and roast everything well, crushing the heads to release their flavor.
3. Add the cognac or brandy and let the alcohol evaporate.
4. Cover with ice, or a mixture of ice and water. The stronger the thermal shock, the better the extraction.
5. Cook for about 1 hour, then strain.
6. For maximum flavor and body, blend the cooked mixture with an immersion blender before straining.

### Cuisine: Western, Italian
