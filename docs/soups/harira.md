# Harira
{: .no_toc }
#### Recreating a soup from Fes

---

## Ingredients
<ul>
	<li>170g chickpeas, soaked</li>
	<li>90g French, green, or brown lentils, soaked</li>
	<li>4 medium-small tomatoes</li>
	<li>1 large onion, quartered</li>
	<li>Celery leaves equal in quantity to the parsley (or use stems if leaves unavailable), chopped</li>
	<li>½ + ¼ bunch of parsley, chopped</li>
	<li>½ + ¼ bunch of cilantro, chopped</li>
	<li>Spoonful of msmen (fermented butter, can replace with ghee)</li>
	<li>1 tbs ground</li>
	<li>1 tbs turmeric</li>
	<li>½ tsp ground black pepper</li>
	<li>Small pinch of cinnamon (optional)</li>
	<li>Small handful of red meat or soup bones from beef or goat (optional)</li>
	<li>⅛ c. olive oil</li>
	<li>Beef broth</li>
	<li>1 small handful of vermicelli or angel hair pasta, crumbled into 1-2" pieces</li>
	<li>5 tbs flour</li>
	<li>1-2 tbs tomato paste</li>
	<li>Lemon (optional, for garnish)</li>
</ul>

## Instructions

In a small pot, put the whole tomatoes and quartered onion. If using celery stalks instead of leaves, also add the stalks. Add a bit of water to the bottom of the pot to steam the vegetables, and set on medium heat until all the vegetables are soft enough to be puréed. Drain the water from the pot. Purée all the cooked vegetables together.

In a large pot, put the chickpeas, puréed vegetables, ½ bunches of parsley and cilantro, celery leaves if using, butter, olive oil, spices, and any meat. Add beef broth and salt. There should be a lot of water relative to the quantity of solids in the soup. Cook until chickpeas are mostly cooked.

Add the lentils to the soup. Adjust water as needed

Put the tomato paste and flour into a small bowl. Whisk 1.5 c. water into the flour, slowly adding the water to prevent clumping. Once lentils are half cooked, add the flour mixture to the soup. Add the vermicelli.

Cook soup for 10-15 more minutes or until lentils and chickpeas are soft, whichever is longer. The vermicelli should be overcooked instead of al dente.

In a mortar and pestle, crush the remaining quarter bunches of parsley and cilantro with a generous pinch of salt. Crush only until flavor is released and the leaves are still visible.

Mix the fresh parsley and cilatro into the soup just before serving. Squeeze lemon on top to taste.

## Notes
<ul>
	<li>The finished soup should be red (from tomatoes) rather than yellow or brown</li>
	<li>The soup should have the consistency of cream. Be careful with the water to flour ratio!</li>
	<li>Regarding the quantity of broth: There should be enough liquid in the soup that, after serving, one does not see all the solids in the bowl and must stir or scoop from the bottom to see the chickpeas.</li>
	<li>The eastern region of Morocco also adds caraway seeds and paprika when adding the spices.</li>
