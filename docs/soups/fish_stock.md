# Fish stock
{: .no_toc }

*Source: Guglielmo G.*

## Ingredients

- Onion, carrot, and celery, roughly chopped
- Olive oil
- Fish bones and heads, chopped into manageable pieces
- 1 glass white wine
- Water
- Salt, about 1–2% by weight
- Whole peppercorns and bay leaves (optional)

## Instructions

1. Cook the onion, carrot, and celery in olive oil.
2. Add the fish bones and heads. Raise the heat to high and roast until they take on some color.
3. Pour in the white wine and allow the alcohol to evaporate for a couple of minutes.
4. Add enough water to cover the bones, season with salt, and add peppercorns or bay leaves if using.
5. Simmer for about 1 hour, then strain.

For a simple appetizer, serve the strained stock with freshly squeezed lemon juice.

### Cuisine: Western, Italian
