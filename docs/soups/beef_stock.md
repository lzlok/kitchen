# Beef stock
{: .no_toc }

*Source: Guglielmo G.*

## Ingredients

- Onions
- Carrots
- Celery
- Fatty, bony beef with connective tissue, such as beef short ribs
- Water
- Salt, about 1–2% by weight

## Instructions

Combine the vegetables, beef, water, and salt. Simmer for 1–1½ hours. Beef short ribs are an especially good choice because they bring fat, bone, and connective tissue to the stock.

### Cuisine: Western, Italian
