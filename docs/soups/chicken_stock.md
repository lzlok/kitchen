# Chicken stock
{: .no_toc }

*Source: Guglielmo G.*

## Ingredients

- 1 whole chicken
- Fatty, bony beef with connective tissue, such as beef short ribs
- Onions
- Carrots
- Celery
- Whole peppercorns
- 1 bay leaf
- Water
- Salt, about 1–2% by weight

## Instructions

1. Cut an onion in half and grill the flat side to add depth and color.
2. Combine it with the chicken, beef, carrots, celery, peppercorns, bay leaf, water, and salt.
3. During the first 10–15 minutes, skim away the foam and impurities released by the chicken and beef.
4. Cook over very low heat until reduced by about one third, ideally for 3 hours or more. For a quicker stock, cook for no less than 1½–2 hours.
5. To remove excess chicken fat, either refrigerate the stock and lift off the solidified fat, or strain the warm stock through a sieve lined with a paper towel. The second method produces a particularly clear, golden broth.

### Cuisine: Western, Italian
