# Vegetable stock
{: .no_toc }

*Source: Guglielmo G.*

## Ingredients

- Onions
- Carrots
- Celery
- Water
- Salt, about 1–2% by weight
- Potatoes, halved (optional)

## Instructions

Combine the onions, carrots, and celery with water and salt. Simmer for about 1 hour. For a little more depth, add potatoes cut in half.

### Cuisine: Western, Italian
