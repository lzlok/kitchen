# Sausage and Porcini Risotto
{: .no_toc }

A hearty saffron risotto with crumbled fresh sausage and porcini mushrooms.

## Ingredients

### About 4 servings

- At least 2 liters hot [vegetable stock]({{ '/docs/soups/vegetable_stock/' | relative_url }}) or [beef stock]({{ '/docs/soups/beef_stock/' | relative_url }})
- 1 generous piece fresh sausage
- ½ yellow onion
- 100 g porcini mushrooms, dried or preferably fresh
- Saffron, as needed
- Carnaroli rice, 1 generous handful per person
- Grana Padano, grated, as needed
- Extra-virgin olive oil, as needed
- Salt, as needed

## Instructions

1. If using dried porcini, soak them in hot water for about 20 minutes. Drain, then slice them thinly. If using fresh porcini, clean and slice them.
2. Finely chop the onion. Heat a generous amount of olive oil in a wide nonstick pan and cook the onion until golden.
3. Remove the sausage from its casing and crumble it by hand into the pan. Cook gently over low heat, breaking it into smaller pieces as it browns.
4. Add the rice and toast it with the sausage and onion, stirring until the grains are thoroughly heated and lightly coated with the cooking fat.
5. Add enough hot stock to loosen the rice, followed by the saffron. Continue cooking, stirring often and adding more stock as it is absorbed.
6. Add enough of the sliced porcini to suit the risotto and continue cooking until the rice is tender but still has a little bite.
7. Taste and adjust the salt. Remove from the heat and stir in a little grated Grana Padano before serving.

### Cuisine: Western, Italian
