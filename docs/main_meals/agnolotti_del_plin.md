# Agnolotti del plin al brasato
{: .no_toc }

<img class="cookbook-photo" src="{{ '/assets/images/cookbook/plin.png' | relative_url }}" alt="Agnolotti del plin">

*Agnolotti del plin* are small, pocket-shaped ravioli typical of Piemonte. Traditionally they contain a rich roasted-meat filling; this version uses brasato.

## Ingredients

### About 5 servings

#### For the pasta

- 300 g flour
- 200 g semolina flour
- 2 whole eggs
- 6 egg yolks

#### For the filling

- 300 g beef suitable for roasting or braising
- 300 g pork shoulder
- 1 carrot
- 1 onion
- 1 celery stalk
- ½ bottle red wine
- 1 bunch spinach
- Rosemary, sage, and thyme, as needed
- 1 egg
- 1 cup heavy cream
- Parmigiano, as needed
- Salt and pepper
- Olive oil

## Instructions

### Roast the meat

1. Sear the beef and pork with olive oil, then set the meat aside.
2. Add the coarsely chopped onion, carrot, celery, and herbs. Cook until they begin to color.
3. Return the meat to the pan and deglaze with the red wine. Cook uncovered over medium heat for at least 15 minutes to remove the alcohol, then add half a bottle of water.
4. Transfer to an ovenproof dish, seal with aluminum foil, and cook at 180°C (350°F) for about 2 hours. Alternatively, cover the pan and finish it on the stove over the lowest heat for about 2 hours.

### Make the filling

1. When the meat is fork-tender, separate it from the sauce. Strain the sauce and reduce it in a saucepan to a demi-glace consistency.
2. Boil the spinach for a couple of minutes, then squeeze it very well to remove excess water.
3. Cut the meat into chunks and blend it with the spinach.
4. Add salt, pepper, Parmigiano, the egg, heavy cream, and reduced sauce. Mix well. The filling should be compact but soft and smooth.
5. Transfer the filling to a pastry bag and refrigerate; it can rest overnight.

### Make the agnolotti

Make the agnolotti with the pasta and filling. The source cookbook does not include separate shaping or cooking instructions.

### Cuisine: Western, Italian, Piedmontese
