# La carbonara
{: .no_toc }

*Source: Guglielmo G.*

<img class="cookbook-photo" src="{{ '/assets/images/cookbook/carbonara2.png' | relative_url }}" alt="La carbonara">

## Ingredients

### Per person

- 120 g pasta, either spaghettoni or mezze maniche
- 1½ egg yolks
- 1–2 thick slices guanciale
- Coarsely ground black pepper, as needed
- Pecorino Romano and Parmigiano, as needed

## Instructions

Start cooking the pasta while you prepare the sauce.

### Cook the guanciale

1. Cut the guanciale into thick cubes or strips and place it in a cold frying pan.
2. Turn the heat to medium-low and let the fat render. As it melts, collect the rendered fat in a cup and continue cooking the guanciale.
3. Do not let the guanciale become completely crisp. It is ready when the fat is transparent and soft with just a little crispness around the edges.

### Make the egg mixture

1. Put the yolks in a large bowl. Add enough of a 50–50 mixture of Pecorino and Parmigiano to make a fairly thick paste—about two handfuls.
2. Add the black pepper; pan-toast it first if you like.
3. Slowly add the reserved guanciale fat while mixing vigorously, as if making mayonnaise. Set aside until the pasta is ready.

### Finish

1. When the pasta is cooked, drain it and immediately transfer it to the egg mixture. Stir vigorously so the heat of the pasta does not scramble the yolks.
2. After 10–20 seconds, a smooth, thick sauce should coat the pasta. Add a very small amount of pasta water if needed.
3. Fold in the guanciale and serve.

### Cuisine: Western, Italian
