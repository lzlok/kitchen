# Pumpkin Risotto with Amaretti
{: .no_toc }

*Source: Ambrogio Maestri.*

A winter risotto that balances sweet pumpkin with savory cheese and a crisp amaretti finish.

## Ingredients

- 250 g Carnaroli rice
- 400 g pumpkin, cut into small cubes
- 50 g butter
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- About 100 g Parmigiano Reggiano or Grana Padano, grated
- 1 generous glass dry white wine
- Crushed amaretti cookies, to taste (optional, but don't substitute for different cookies)
- Extra-virgin olive oil, as needed
- Salt, as needed
- Hot [vegetable stock]({{ '/docs/soups/vegetable_stock/' | relative_url }}), as needed (stock made with a bouillon cube is also fine)

## Instructions

1. Bring a saucepan of lightly salted water to a boil. Add the diced pumpkin and cook for a few minutes, just until it begins to soften. Drain and set aside.
2. Heat a little olive oil in a wide pan. Add the onion and cook gently until soft and its moisture has evaporated.
3. Add the rice, raise the heat, and toast for 2–3 minutes, stirring so every grain becomes hot.
4. Pour in the white wine and let it evaporate completely.
5. Fold in the partially cooked pumpkin. Add a ladleful of hot vegetable stock and stir.
6. Continue cooking for about 20 minutes, stirring continuously or very often so the rice does not stick to the bottom, and adding more hot stock whenever it begins to look dry. The pumpkin should soften into the risotto as it cooks.
7. When the rice is tender but still has a little bite, turn off the heat. Stir in the butter and grated cheese until creamy, then fold in some crushed amaretti for a contrasting crunch.
8. Serve with an extra scattering of crushed amaretti. For a festive presentation, spoon the risotto into a hollowed-out pumpkin.

### Cuisine: Western, Italian
