# Scaloppine al vino rosso
{: .no_toc }

*Source: Guglielmo G.*

*Scaloppine* are a popular quick dinner of thin cutlets that are floured, seared, and deglazed with wine. Veal makes the most delicious version, but pork, beef, or chicken will also work.

## Ingredients

### Per person

- 3–4 veal cutlets
- 1 glass red wine
- Olive oil and butter
- Flour, as needed
- Parsley or sage
- Salt

## Instructions

1. **Flour the cutlets:** Coat the cutlets lightly with flour.
2. **Sear:** Add a generous amount of olive oil and butter to a wide frying pan and heat over medium to medium-high. If using sage, add it now; save parsley for the end.
3. Put in the cutlets and let them sear for a few minutes without moving them too much. Season with salt. After about 3 minutes, check that they are well browned and flip. Add more olive oil if the pan looks dry, then raise the heat to high.
4. **Deglaze and finish:** About 30 seconds after flipping, pour in the wine. Let it evaporate briefly, then turn the heat to low and cook until a dark, glossy sauce forms.
5. Serve warm, topped with finely chopped parsley if using.

## Variations

Deglaze with white wine, port, Madeira, Marsala, freshly squeezed lemon juice, or a mixture of white wine and lemon juice.

### Cuisine: Western, Italian
