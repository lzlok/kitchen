# Ragù alla bolognese
{: .no_toc }

*Source: Guglielmo G.*

<img class="cookbook-photo" src="{{ '/assets/images/cookbook/ragu.png' | relative_url }}" alt="Tagliatelle with ragù alla bolognese">

## Ingredients

- 1 kg ground meat, half pork and half beef
- 1 sweet sausage
- 2 carrots
- 1 onion
- 2 celery stalks
- 1 glass red wine
- 1 small jar tomato passata
- Rosemary, as needed
- ½ glass milk
- Olive oil
- Salt

## Instructions

1. Begin a soffritto with the very finely chopped onion, carrots, and celery. Cook over low heat for 5–10 minutes.
2. Remove the sausage casing and add the sausage in small pieces, breaking it up further with a wooden spoon as it cooks.
3. After a few minutes, add all the ground meat and raise the heat to high. Season generously with salt.
4. When the meat is browned, add the red wine. Cook for 2–3 minutes, then add the passata.
5. Add enough water to fully cover the meat, plus a little more, and add the rosemary.
6. Turn the heat to its lowest setting, cover, and cook for 3 hours.
7. Stir in the milk, taste, and adjust the salt. Serve with tagliatelle or use in lasagna.

### Cuisine: Western, Italian, Bolognese
