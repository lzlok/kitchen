# Spaghetti allo scoglio
{: .no_toc }

*Source: Guglielmo G.*

<img class="cookbook-photo" src="{{ '/assets/images/cookbook/scoglio.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="Spaghetti allo scoglio with clams">

## Ingredients

### About 4 servings

- 500 g linguine or spaghetti
- 1 kg clams
- 5–6 cherry tomatoes
- 1 clove garlic
- White wine, as needed
- Parsley, as needed
- Olive oil

## Instructions

### Wash the clams

If you have time, put the clams in a bowl of heavily salted water and refrigerate for at least 2 hours, changing the water every 30 minutes. If you do not have time, the settling method below will keep sand out of the dish. Most commercially sold, farm-raised clams contain little sand.

### Cook the clams

1. Add olive oil and the smashed garlic clove to a large frying pan. Cook over medium heat until the garlic turns golden.
2. Add the halved cherry tomatoes and cook over medium-low heat for 2–3 minutes, pressing them gently with a spatula to help form a sauce.
3. Raise the heat to high and add the clams. Let the pan heat for about 1 minute, then deglaze with a full glass of white wine and add half a glass of water.
4. Cover and cook for 2–3 minutes, or just until the clams begin to open. Remove the pan from the heat.
5. Take the clam meat from the shells, discard the shells, and set the meat aside.
6. If the clams were purged beforehand, leave the cooking liquid in the pan. Otherwise, pour the liquid into a tall glass or bowl and let it settle for 20 minutes, then carefully pour it back into the pan without disturbing the sand at the bottom.

### Cook the pasta

1. Boil the pasta for about half of its usual cooking time. Drain and transfer it to the clam liquid.
2. Finish cooking over high heat, stirring often to prevent sticking and release enough starch to make the sauce creamy.
3. When the pasta is almost done, return the clams to the pan and add a generous amount of finely chopped parsley.
4. Finish off the heat with fresh olive oil.

### Cuisine: Western, Italian
