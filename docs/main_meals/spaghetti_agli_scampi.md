# Spaghetti agli scampi
{: .no_toc }

## Ingredients

### Per person

- 120 g spaghetti
- 4 scampi
- 5–10 cherry tomatoes
- Garlic, as needed
- Tomato concentrate, as needed
- Carrot, onion, and celery, as needed
- White wine or cognac, as needed
- Parsley, as needed
- Olive oil

## Instructions

### Make the scampi bisque

1. Clean the scampi, separating the heads from the bodies and removing the shells. Set the meat aside.
2. Make the [crustacean bisque]({{ '/docs/soups/seafood_stock/#crustacean-bisque' | relative_url }}) with the scampi heads and shells. Strain and set aside.

### Make the sauce

1. Add olive oil and a smashed garlic clove to a frying pan. Cook until the garlic turns golden.
2. Add the halved cherry tomatoes and cook over medium to medium-high heat for a few minutes, until fork-tender. Press them gently with a spatula to help form the sauce.
3. Cut the scampi meat into small cubes and add it to the sauce. Cook over low heat for a few minutes, then set aside.

### Cook the pasta in the bisque

1. Boil the spaghetti for about half of its usual cooking time.
2. Transfer it to the tomato sauce and add enough bisque to barely cover the pasta, as you would for a risotto.
3. Cook over medium-high heat, stirring often to prevent sticking and help create a starchy sauce. Add more bisque as needed until the pasta is done. This method usually takes 3–4 minutes longer than boiling alone.
4. Serve with finely chopped parsley.

### Cuisine: Western, Italian
