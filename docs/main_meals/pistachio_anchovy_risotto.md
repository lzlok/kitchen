# Pistachio Risotto with Anchovies and Crisp Spring Onions
{: .no_toc }

#### Created by Andrea Berton

*Source: Guida Gallo.*

A refined seafood risotto topped with lemon-marinated anchovies, smooth pistachio cream, and slowly dried spring onions.

## Ingredients

### About 4 servings

- 280 g Carnaroli del Pavese Gran Riserva rice
- 2 liters hot [fish stock]({{ '/docs/soups/fish_stock/' | relative_url }})
- ½ glass white wine
- 50 g butter
- 8 fresh anchovies
- 4 basil leaves
- 1 lemon
- 2 young garlic cloves (listed in the original recipe; their use is not specified)
- 4 spring onions
- Caster sugar, as needed
- 80 g pistachios
- Extra-virgin olive oil, including 80 g for the pistachio cream
- Salt and pepper

## Instructions

### Marinate the anchovies

1. Remove the anchovy heads and innards. Wash the fish carefully under running water, then remove the bones while leaving each pair of fillets joined at the tail.
2. Pour a thin layer of olive oil onto a small plate. Add two drops of lemon juice and the torn basil leaves.
3. Lay the anchovies in the marinade for 30 minutes. Turn them and marinate for another 30 minutes.

### Prepare the spring onions

1. Wash and trim the spring onions, removing the green tops, then slice them thinly.
2. Steam for 3 minutes. Season with caster sugar, salt, and pepper.
3. Spread on a baking sheet and dry in an 80°C (175°F) oven for 2 hours, until crisp.

### Make the pistachio cream

Lightly toast the pistachios. In a Thermomix or another temperature-controlled high-speed blender, blend them with 80 g extra-virgin olive oil until completely smooth, warming the mixture to about 82°C (180°F) as it blends. Chill to 4°C (40°F) and reserve.

### Cook and serve the risotto

1. Heat a little olive oil in a wide pan. Add the rice and stir until every grain is hot and coated.
2. Add the white wine and let it evaporate.
3. Add the hot fish stock one ladleful at a time, stirring and allowing each addition to absorb before adding the next. Continue until the rice is tender but still has a little bite.
4. Remove from the heat. Stir in a little olive oil and a knob of the butter until creamy.
5. Spread the risotto across four flat plates. Top each serving with two marinated anchovies, pistachio cream, and the crisp spring onions.

### Cuisine: Western, Italian
