# Paccheri allo scorfano
{: .no_toc }

*Source: Guglielmo G.*

<img class="cookbook-photo" src="{{ '/assets/images/cookbook/scorfano2.png' | relative_url }}" alt="Paccheri allo scorfano">

## Ingredients

### About 5 servings

- 600 g paccheri
- 1 whole scorfano (rockfish)
- 500 g cherry tomatoes
- 1–2 shallots
- ½ glass white wine
- Parsley, as needed
- Olive oil
- Salt
- Anchovies (optional, preferably from the Cantabrian Sea)
- Bottarga (optional, the best is from mullet)

## Instructions

1. Fillet the fish. Keep the head and center spine to flavor the sauce, and cut the fillets into ½-inch cubes.
2. Thinly slice the shallots. Cook them with olive oil in a large frying pan until softened.
3. Add the fish head and bones, then raise the heat to medium-high. After a few minutes, turn the heat to high and deglaze with the white wine.
4. Let the wine evaporate completely, then add the halved cherry tomatoes. Reduce the heat to medium and season with salt.
5. When the tomatoes are fork-tender, press them gently with a spatula to help form the sauce. Remove the fish head and bones.
6. Add the cubed fish, lower the heat, cover, and cook for just 2–3 minutes.
7. For the deepest flavor, let the finished sauce rest for 1 hour.
8. Boil the paccheri, drain, and toss with the sauce. It's best to cook half the time in the pasta water and half the time in the sauce. You can add the anchovies and crush them into the sauce at this point. Serve with a generous amount of finely chopped parsley and optionally grated bottarga.

### Cuisine: Western, Italian
