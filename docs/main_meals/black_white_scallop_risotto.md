# Black and White Risotto with Saffron, Scallops, and Hazelnut Oil
{: .no_toc }

#### Created by Marco Cavallucci and Angelo Asirelli

*Source: Guida Gallo.*

Two contrasting risottos—creamy Carnaroli and nutty black rice—finished with saffron, grilled scallops, and hazelnut oil.

## Ingredients

### About 4 servings

- 140 g Carnaroli del Pavese Gran Riserva rice
- 140 g black Venus rice
- 12 scallops
- 1 shallot, finely chopped and divided
- Abruzzese saffron threads, as needed
- Hot [fish stock]({{ '/docs/soups/fish_stock/' | relative_url }}), as needed
- Hazelnut oil or chopped hazelnuts
- Extra-virgin olive oil, as needed
- White wine, as needed
- Cervia sea salt or another delicate sea salt, as needed
- Black pepper, as needed

## Instructions

### Cook the Carnaroli risotto

1. Soften half the chopped shallot in a little olive oil.
2. Add the Carnaroli rice and toast, stirring until every grain is hot and coated.
3. Add the white wine and let it evaporate.
4. Cook the rice by adding hot fish stock a little at a time, stirring until the rice is tender but still has a little bite.
5. Remove from the heat and stir in hazelnut oil until creamy.

### Cook the black rice

1. Boil the Venus rice in lightly salted water for 10 minutes, then drain.
2. Soften the remaining shallot in a little olive oil in a separate saucepan. Add the drained black rice and finish cooking it with hot fish stock.
3. Remove from the heat and stir in hazelnut oil until creamy.

### Grill the scallops and serve

1. Clean and wash the scallops. Grill until just cooked and season with Cervia sea salt and black pepper.
2. Soften the saffron threads in a spoonful of boiling fish stock.
3. Place a small mound of black rice in the center of each plate and surround it with the Carnaroli risotto.
4. Garnish with the softened saffron threads and arrange three grilled scallops over each serving.

### Cuisine: Western, Italian
