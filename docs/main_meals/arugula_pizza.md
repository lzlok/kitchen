# Arugula Prosciutto Pizza
{: .no_toc }
---

## Ingredients
#### From <a href-"https://thepracticalkitchen.com/arugula-and-prosciutto-pizza/#recipe" target="_blank">The Practical Kitchen</a>

### Olive Oil Sauce
<ul>
	<li>Olive oil</li>
	<li>¼ c. shallots, minced</li>
	<li>1 clove of garlic, minced</li>
	<li>1 sprig of fresh rosemary, minced</li>
	<li>⅛ tsp. salt</li>
	<li>⅛ tsp. black pepper</li>
</ul>

### Toppings
<ul>
	<li>3 slices prosciutto, torn</li>
	<li>1½ c. baby arugula</li>
	<li>1½ tbs. lemon juice (about half a lemon)</li>
	<li>½ tsp. salt</li>
	<li>¾ c. low-moisture mozzarella, grated</li>
	<li>1 tbs. parmesan cheese, finely grated</li>
</ul>

## Instructions

### Sauce

In a bowl, mix all the sauce incredients. Add just enough olive oil to be level with the mixture otherwise the pizza becomes too oily.

### Assembly

Spread the olive oil mixture over the stretched raw pizza dough. Top with grated mozzarella and parmesan cheese. Sprinkle the torn prosciutto on the pizza, letting it drape and tent so that lots of edges and corners point up toward the broiler.

While pizza is cooking, mix the arugula with lemon juice, salt, and pepper.

Broil the pizza for the final 30-60 seconds. As soon as the pizza comes out of the oven, top with arugula.
