# Capretto al vino rosso di Paola
{: .no_toc }

My mother's baby goat recipe. No additional words are needed: this is it.

## Ingredients

- ½ baby goat, butchered into 2–3 inch pieces
- 1 bottle everyday red wine, for the marinade
- Up to 1 bottle better red wine, for cooking—Barolo, Barbaresco, Rosso di Montalcino, or a good Chianti
- Vegetable broth, as needed
- Olive oil and butter
- Rosemary and sage
- Bay leaves, peppercorns, onion, carrot, and celery (optional, for the marinade)
- Salt

## Instructions

### Marinate

The night before cooking, put the goat in a bowl with the everyday red wine and rosemary. You can also add bay leaves, peppercorns, and coarsely chopped onion, carrot, and celery.

### Sear

1. The next day, remove the goat from the marinade and dry the pieces well.
2. Pour olive oil into a large, wide pan and heat over medium to medium-high.
3. Arrange the goat in a single layer without crowding. The pieces can touch, but do not squeeze them in or stack them; a good sear is the most important part.
4. Brown the pieces well on all sides, then pour in the better wine, using less than the full bottle if needed.

### Braise

1. Let the wine evaporate for a few minutes, then reduce the heat to low. Season with salt and add rosemary and sage.
2. Cover the pan, leaving the lid about 10% open, and cook for at least 1½ hours. Add a little stock if the liquid evaporates too quickly.
3. Turn the pieces occasionally so they cook evenly. The goat is done when the liquid has almost completely evaporated into a fairly thick, dark sauce.

### Finish

Turn off the heat and keep the goat covered until serving. Just before serving, warm it over medium-high heat and add a few tablespoons of butter to caramelize the meat and give it a glossy, sticky finish.

## Variation

Use the same recipe for rabbit, but skip the overnight marinade.
