# L'amatriciana
{: .no_toc }

## Ingredients

### About 4 servings

- 500 g mezze maniche or another pasta
- 1 large jar tomato passata
- 6 thick slices guanciale
- Pecorino Romano, as needed
- Black pepper, as needed
- Olive oil
- Salt

## Instructions

1. **Make the tomato sauce:** Reduce the passata with a little salt until it changes from very liquid to rich and dense, about 10 minutes. Finish with a little olive oil and plenty of ground black pepper.
2. **Cook the guanciale:** Cut the guanciale into chunky strips and cook in a pan. When it has rendered its fat and is beginning to crisp, but is not fully crisp, add the guanciale and all its fat to the tomato sauce. Mix well and continue cooking over low heat.
3. **Cook the pasta:** Boil the pasta until al dente.
4. **Finish:** Combine the pasta and sauce. Mix well for a couple of minutes, adding a little pasta water if needed to adjust the consistency. Off the heat, add as much grated Pecorino as your heart desires.

### Cuisine: Western, Italian
