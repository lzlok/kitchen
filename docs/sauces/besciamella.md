# Besciamella
{: .no_toc }

*Source: Guglielmo G.*

## Ingredients

- 100 g butter
- 100 g flour
- 1 liter cold whole milk

## Instructions

1. Make a roux by melting the butter, adding the flour, and cooking over medium heat for 1–2 minutes while whisking.
2. Pour in the cold milk and keep whisking to prevent lumps.
3. Continue cooking over medium heat, stirring constantly, until the sauce begins to boil, about 5–10 minutes.
4. Cook until smooth, silky, and thickened, but not overly dense.

### Cuisine: Western, Italian
