# Mayonnaise
{: .no_toc }

*Source: Guglielmo G.*

Mayonnaise is an emulsion of egg yolks and oil. Use a neutral oil: grapeseed is ideal, with canola or avocado oil as good alternatives.

## Ingredients

- 2–3 egg yolks
- Grapeseed, canola, or avocado oil, as needed
- Lemon juice
- 1 tablespoon boiling water (optional)

## Instructions

1. Place the yolks in a metal bowl and add a few drops of lemon juice. The acidity adds flavor and helps stabilize the emulsion.
2. Whisk steadily while pouring in the oil very slowly. Adding it too quickly can break the sauce.
3. Once the sauce becomes thicker and paler, increase the rate at which you add the oil.
4. Stop when the mayonnaise forms thick, firm peaks on the whisk. Add a few more drops of lemon juice and, if desired, a tablespoon of boiling water.
