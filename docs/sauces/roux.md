# Roux
{: .no_toc }

*Source: Guglielmo G.*

A roux is a cooked paste of flour and butter used to thicken sauces. Adjust the ratio and cooking time to change its thickening power and color.

## Ingredients

- Butter
- An equal weight of flour

## Instructions

Melt the butter in a saucepan. Add all the flour and cook over medium heat for 1–2 minutes, stirring with a whisk. Cook longer when a blonde or brown roux is needed.

### Cuisine: Western, French
