# Hollandaise
{: .no_toc }

Hollandaise is an emulsion of egg yolks and butter. It is especially good over eggs or asparagus.

## Ingredients

- 4 egg yolks
- 250 g butter, melted
- Salt
- Lemon juice

## Instructions

1. Bring a little water to a simmer in a small saucepan and place a heatproof bowl over it to make a bain-marie.
2. Add the yolks to the bowl and begin whisking. From this point on, keep the sauce moving constantly.
3. After about 1 minute, when the yolks are slightly paler and thicker, begin pouring in the butter very slowly while whisking.
4. Remove the bowl from the bain-marie whenever it feels too hot; the eggs must not cook. Continue whisking and adding butter until the sauce is thick and pale yellow.
5. Finish with salt and a generous squeeze of lemon juice.

### Cuisine: Western, French
